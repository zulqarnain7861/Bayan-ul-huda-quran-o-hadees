1. OVERVIEW & ZERO-ADVERTISING COMMITMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Bayan al-Huda ("the App") is a 100% free, ad-free Islamic educational and worship companion application providing the Holy Quran (Arabic script, translations, Tafsirs, and audio recitations), Sunni and Shia Hadith collections, 5 Daily Salah Prayer Times, Sehri/Iftar Timetables, Azan and Fajr Alarms, Qibla Compass, Masnoon Duas, and Islamic scholarly lectures.

We respect your privacy:
- The App contains NO third-party advertising SDKs.
- We do NOT sell, rent, trade, or monetize any user data.
- You can use the App in a 7-Day Renewable Offline Guest Mode without creating or signing into an account.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. INFORMATION WE COLLECT AND PROCESS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Depending on which features you choose to use, the App processes the following categories of information:

A. Account & Authentication Data (Optional — Only When You Sign In with Google)
- Service Used: Google Sign-In via Android Jetpack Credential Manager (`androidx.credentials`) and Google Firebase Authentication (`com.google.firebase:firebase-auth`).
- Data Processed:
  1. Email address associated with your Google Account
  2. Display name associated with your Google Account
  3. Profile photo URL (if provided by Google)
  4. Firebase Authentication User ID (`uid`)
- Purpose: To authenticate your identity and allow you to synchronize your personal Quran/Hadith bookmarks and study notes across devices.
- Guest Mode Alternative: If you select "Continue as Guest", no Google account data is collected or transmitted to Firebase Authentication.

B. User-Generated Study Data (Cloud Sync via Firebase Firestore)
- Service Used: Google Cloud Firestore (`com.google.firebase:firebase-firestore`).
- Firestore Database Collections:
  1. `/users/{userId}/bookmarks/{bookmarkId}` — Stores bookmark type (`AYAH` or `HADITH`), scripture reference key, title, Arabic snippet, translation snippet, and creation timestamp.
  2. `/users/{userId}/notes/{noteId}` — Stores personal study note title, text content, scripture reference key, item type, and creation/update timestamps.
- Purpose: Cloud backup and multi-device synchronization of your personal bookmarks and study notes when signed in with Google.
- Local-Only Mode: In Guest Mode, bookmarks and study notes are stored exclusively on your device inside the local SQLite Room database (`bayan_ul_huda_v8.db`).

C. Location Data (Approximate & Precise Location)
- Android Permissions: `android.permission.ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION` and `android.permission.ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION`.
- How Location Is Used:
  1. Calculating accurate local 5 Daily Salah Prayer Times and Ramadan Sehri/Iftar Timetables.
  2. Calculating the Qibla direction angle and distance from your current coordinates to the Holy Kaaba in Makkah (21.4225° N, 39.8262° E).
  3. Querying prayer timetable coordinates from the public AlAdhan Prayer Times API (`https://api.aladhan.com/v1/`).
- Foreground & On-Demand Only: Location is accessed only when you actively use location detection in the Prayer Times, Sehri/Iftar Timetable, or Qibla Compass screens. The App does NOT request `ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION` and does NOT track your movement in the background.
- Manual City Fallback: Granting location permission is optional. You can select your city or coordinates manually without granting GPS access.

D. Microphone & Voice Search Input (`RECORD_AUDIO`)
- Android Permission: `android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO`.
- How Audio Input Is Used:
  - Accessed ONLY when you explicitly tap the microphone icon in the Search screen to dictate a search query using Android's system SpeechRecognizer service.
- No Recording Storage or Upload:
  - Bayan al-Huda does NOT record, save, archive, or upload any voice or microphone audio files to any developer server or cloud database.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. OFFLINE DOWNLOADS & DEVICE STORAGE (NO EXTERNAL STORAGE PERMISSIONS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Zero Broad Storage Permissions: Bayan al-Huda does NOT request `READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE`, `WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE`, `READ_MEDIA_IMAGES`, `READ_MEDIA_AUDIO`, `READ_MEDIA_VIDEO`, or `MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE`.
- Sandboxed Internal Storage: All downloaded Quran translations, Hadith collections, SQLite databases (`bayan_ul_huda_v8.db` and `bayan_knowledge_v1.db`), and cached MP3 Quran recitations are stored strictly inside Android's private application-specific directories (`context.filesDir` and `context.cacheDir`).
- User Control: You can clear cached Quran audio at any time via Info/About -> Storage Cleaner ("Clear Cached Quran Audio") or remove offline packages in the Offline Library screen.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. DEVICE PERMISSIONS SUMMARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The App declares the following permissions in `AndroidManifest.xml` solely for core functionality:
1. `INTERNET` & `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`: Fetching Quran translations/tafsirs, Hadith collections, AlAdhan prayer schedules, Quran audio recitations, YouTube lecture streams, and Firebase cloud sync.
2. `ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION` & `ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION`: On-demand GPS coordinate lookup for local Prayer Times, Sehri/Iftar timetables, and Qibla Compass direction.
3. `RECORD_AUDIO`: On-demand voice-to-text search input when tapping the microphone icon.
4. `POST_NOTIFICATIONS`: Displaying 5 Daily Salah Azan notifications, pre-Fajr wake-up alarm notifications, and daily Sunnah/Dua reminders.
5. `SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM`, `USE_EXACT_ALARM`, & `USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT`: Ensuring Salah Azan alerts and the Fajr wake-up alarm trigger at the exact astronomical prayer time.
6. `RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED`: Rescheduling active Salah Azan and Fajr alarms after your device restarts.
7. `FOREGROUND_SERVICE` & `FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK`: Playing Quran audio recitations and full Azan audio reliably while the screen is off or when switching apps (`AzanForegroundService`).
8. `VIBRATE` & `WAKE_LOCK`: Waking the device and vibrating during scheduled Fajr and Salah alarms.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES & CONTENT APIS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
To deliver verified Islamic texts, audio recitations, prayer times, and lectures, the App communicates over encrypted HTTPS connections with:
- Google Firebase Authentication & Cloud Firestore (Authentication & optional bookmark/note cloud sync)
- Al-Quran Cloud API (`api.alquran.cloud`), Quran.com API (`api.quran.com`), QuranEnc API (`quranenc.com`), and QUL (`qul.tarteel.ai`) (Quran Arabic text, translations, Tafsirs, and recitations)
- EveryAyah & Islamic Network Audio CDN (`everyayah.com`, `cdn.islamic.network`) (Streamed/cached MP3 Quran recitations)
- Hadith API (`cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/fawazahmed0/hadith-api`) (Sunni & Shia Hadith editions)
- AlAdhan API (`api.aladhan.com`) (Astronomical prayer times & Hijri calendar calculation based on coordinates/city)
- YouTube (`youtube.com`, `youtube-nocookie.com`, `i.ytimg.com`) (Streaming Islamic video lectures by Engineer Muhammad Ali Mirza and displaying video thumbnails)

7. DATA SECURITY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Encryption in Transit: All network requests use HTTPS/TLS encryption.
- Access Control: Cloud Firestore security rules (`firestore.rules`) enforce strict zero-trust ownership (`request.auth != null && request.auth.uid == userId`), ensuring that no other user can read, modify, or delete your synced bookmarks or study notes.
- Input Sanitization: User-submitted bookmarks and study notes are validated and length-bounded before storage.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8. DATA RETENTION & ACCOUNT DELETION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Retention: Your Firebase Authentication account and Cloud Firestore bookmarks/notes are retained only for as long as you maintain your account in the App. Local data remains on your device until you clear it or uninstall the App.
- How to Delete Your Account and Data Inside the App:
  1. Open Bayan al-Huda and tap the Info ("i") icon in the top bar to open the Info / About screen.
  2. In the "Cloud Sync Account (Firebase)" card or the "Privacy, Terms, Permissions & Account Control" section, tap "Delete Account" / "Delete Account & Cloud Data".
  3. Confirm deletion in the dialog. This immediately and permanently deletes:
     - All Cloud Firestore bookmarks (`/users/{uid}/bookmarks`)
     - All Cloud Firestore study notes (`/users/{uid}/notes`)
     - Your Firebase Authentication user account (`FirebaseAuth.getInstance().currentUser.delete()`)
     - Your local bookmarks and study notes on the device (when the checkbox is selected).
