4. PRAYER TIMES, SEHRI/IFTAR TIMETABLES, ALARMS & QIBLA COMPASS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Astronomical Calculations: Prayer times and Sehri/Iftar schedules are calculated using mathematical astronomical formulas and the AlAdhan API based on your selected GPS coordinates or city, juristic school (Shafi'i/Maliki/Hanbali or Hanafi Asr shadow factor), and calculation authority (e.g., University of Islamic Sciences Karachi, ISNA, Muslim World League, Umm al-Qura Makkah, Egyptian General Authority, Shia Ithna-Ashari / Leva Institute Qum).
- Compass Sensor Accuracy: Qibla direction angles rely on your device's internal magnetometer/accelerometer sensors or GPS coordinates. Metallic objects, magnetic cases, or uncalibrated device sensors can affect compass readings.
- User Responsibility: Always allow a prudent precautionary margin for fasting (Sehri/Iftar) and prayer times, and cross-check with your local mosque timetable where appropriate. Device battery savers or Do Not Disturb settings may affect alarm delivery unless exact alarm and notification permissions are enabled.
