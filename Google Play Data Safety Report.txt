================================================================================
PART 1 — HIGH-LEVEL DATA SAFETY QUESTIONS (PLAY CONSOLE STEP 1)
================================================================================

1. "Does your app collect or share any of the required user data types?"
   -> Answer: YES
   (Reason: When a user chooses to sign in with Google, the app collects their
   Name, Email address, User ID, and cloud-synced Quran/Hadith bookmarks & study
   notes in Firebase Auth / Cloud Firestore. When a user requests GPS prayer times,
   approximate/precise location coordinates are sent over HTTPS to the AlAdhan API
   to compute local prayer schedules.)

2. "Is all of the user data collected by your app encrypted in transit?"
   -> Answer: YES
   (Reason: All Firebase Auth, Cloud Firestore, AlAdhan, Quran/Hadith API, and
   YouTube connections use HTTPS/TLS encryption.)

3. "Do you provide a way for users to request that their data is deleted?"
   -> Answer: YES
   - In-app deletion pathway: Info/About screen -> "Delete Account & Cloud Data"
     button (deletes Firebase Auth user record + `/users/{uid}/bookmarks` +
     `/users/{uid}/notes` + local bookmarks/notes).
   - Web deletion URL: Paste your published Google Sites "Account Deletion Policy"
     webpage URL into the Play Console field.

CATEGORY D: AUDIO / MICROPHONE (`RECORD_AUDIO`) — DO NOT MARK AS COLLECTED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. Voice or sound recordings
   - Collected: NO
   - Shared: NO
   - Explanation (Verified from source code):
     Although `android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO` is declared in `AndroidManifest.xml`
     for on-device Voice Search (`SpeechRecognizer`), the app NEVER records, stores,
     or transmits audio files off the device to any developer backend. Under Google
     Play Data Safety rules, data processed strictly on-device or sent only via
     system speech-to-text into a local search bar without off-device audio storage
     does not need to be declared as collected audio recordings.

PART 3 — PERMISSIONS & DECLARATIONS IN PLAY CONSOLE "APP CONTENT"
================================================================================
When submitting the APK/AAB in Play Console, be prepared for these standard
manifest permission prompts:

1. `USE_EXACT_ALARM` / `SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM`:
   - Justification if prompted: "Bayan al-Huda is an Islamic worship, prayer-time
     (Azan), and Fajr wake-up alarm application. Exact alarms are a core user-facing
     feature required to sound the 5 Daily Salah Azan notifications and pre-Fajr
     wake-up alarm at the exact astronomical prayer times."

2. `FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK`:
   - Justification in Play Console Foreground Service declaration:
     - Category: MediaPlayback
     - Description: "Used by AzanForegroundService and Quran Audio Player to play
       scheduled Azan recitations, Fajr wake-up alarm audio, and continuous Quran
       Surah tilawat audio while the user has locked the screen or switched apps."
   - Note: Play Console requires a short screen-recording video URL demonstrating
     the foreground media playback notification in action (e.g., playing Quran audio
     or testing an Azan alarm and showing the media notification in the Android
     notification shade).

3. `USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT`:
   - Justification: "Used for the scheduled Fajr wake-up alarm and Salah Azan alarm
     so the user can dismiss or snooze the alarm when the device screen is locked."
