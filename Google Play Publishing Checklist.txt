Use this step-by-step checklist to complete your Google Play Store submission
outside the app.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 1 — PUBLISH YOUR PUBLIC PAGES ON GOOGLE SITES (FREE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Go to https://sites.google.com and create a new public Google Site named
   "Bayan al-Huda".
2. Create Page 1: "Privacy Policy"
   - Copy and paste the full text from `/Privacy Policy.txt`.
   - Complete the optional address/phone confirmation if needed, and remove the
     top bracketed instruction note.
3. Create Page 2: "Account Deletion"
   - Copy and paste the full text from `/Account Deletion Policy.txt`.
   - Remove the top bracketed instruction note.
4. Create Page 3: "Terms and Conditions"
   - Copy and paste the full text from `/Terms and Conditions.txt`.
   - Confirm your jurisdiction in Section 9 and remove the bracketed note.
5. Click "Publish" in Google Sites and make sure the site visibility is set to
   "Public" (anyone on the web can view without signing in).
6. Copy the public URLs for:
   - Privacy Policy page
   - Account Deletion page
   - Terms & Conditions page

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 2 — GOOGLE PLAY CONSOLE ACCOUNT & APP CREATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Register your Google Play Console Developer Account ($25 one-time fee) at
   https://play.google.com/console and complete identity verification.
2. Click "Create app":
   - App name: Bayan al-Huda
   - Default language: English (United States) — `en-US`
   - App or game: App
   - Free or paid: Free
   - Declarations: Check Developer Program Policies and US export laws.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 3 — COMPLETE "APP CONTENT" DECLARATIONS IN PLAY CONSOLE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Navigate to Policy and programs -> App content and complete every section:

[ ] 1. Privacy Policy:
    - Paste your published Google Sites Privacy Policy URL.
[ ] 2. App Access (Login Credentials for Google Play Reviewers):
    - Select: "All or some functionality in my app is restricted" (or provide
      instructions explaining both options):
      * Option A: "Reviewers can tap 'Continue as Guest (7 Days Offline Access)'
        on the welcome screen to access all Quran, Hadith, Prayer Times, Qibla,
        Duas, Search, and Video Lecture features immediately without any login."
      * Option B: "Reviewers can also tap 'Sign in with Google' using any Google
        account to test Firebase Cloud Sync and Account Deletion (Info 'i' button
        -> Delete Account & Cloud Data)."
[ ] 3. Ads:
    - Select: "No, my app does not contain ads."
[ ] 4. Content Rating (IARC Questionnaire):
    - Category: Reference, News, or Educational
    - Violence / Sexuality / Profanity / Controlled Substances: None
    - User Interaction / Sharing: Users can sync personal private bookmarks/notes to
      their own account (not public social posting).
[ ] 5. Target Audience and Content:
    - Select target age groups: 13–15, 16–17, 18 and over (Recommended so you do
      not trigger Google Play Families Policy restrictions unless you specifically
      intend to join the Designed for Families program).
    - Appeal to children: "No" (general audience religious reference app).
[ ] 6. Data Safety:
    - Follow `/Google Play Data Safety Report.txt` line by line.
    - Paste your published Google Sites Account Deletion URL in the Account
      Deletion URL field.
[ ] 7. Government Apps:
    - Select: "No, this is not a government app."
[ ] 8. Financial Features:
    - Select: "My app doesn't provide any financial features."
[ ] 9. Health Apps:
    - Select: "My app does not provide any health features."
[ ] 10. Foreground Service Permission (`FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK`):
    - Select "Media playback" (Quran Audio Recitation & Azan Alarm playback).
    - Record a short 15–30 second screen recording on your phone showing Quran
      audio or Azan playing with the persistent notification visible, upload it
      as an unlisted YouTube video or Google Drive public link, and paste the
      link in the Foreground Service declaration form.
[ ] 11. Exact Alarm & Full-Screen Intent Permissions (`USE_EXACT_ALARM`, `USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT`):
    - Declare "Alarm / Timer / Religious Prayer Time Reminder (Salah Azan & Fajr
      Wake-up Alarm)" as the core functionality requiring exact time triggers.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 4 — CONFIGURE GOOGLE SIGN-IN SHA-1 / SHA-256 IN FIREBASE FOR PLAY APP SIGNING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
IMPORTANT FOR GOOGLE SIGN-IN ON PLAY STORE:
When you upload an Android App Bundle (`.aab`) or APK to Google Play Console,
Google Play App Signing uses a Google-managed production signing key.
1. In Google Play Console, go to:
   Test and release -> Setup -> App signing.
2. Copy both the **SHA-1** and **SHA-256** certificate fingerprints under
   "App signing key certificate".
3. Open Firebase Console (https://console.firebase.google.com) -> Project
   `gen-lang-client-0124169087` -> Project Settings -> Your Apps (`com.bayanulhuda.com`)
   -> Add fingerprint -> Paste the Play Console SHA-1 and SHA-256 fingerprints.
   (Without this step, Google Sign-In works on debug/local APKs but would fail on
   Play Store downloads.)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  after Nov 13, 2023):
